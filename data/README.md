# Muscle Gain Data

This folder contains a practical dataset for a muscle-building plan centered on:

- weighted compound training
- enough recovery and sleep
- calorie and macro targets for lean bulk
- daily meal structure
- hydration guidance

## Files

- `exercises.csv`: strength exercise selection, sets, reps, and training frequency
- `rest_and_recovery.csv`: sleep, recovery, and recovery-day targets
- `food_plan.csv`: sample daily meal structure
- `macros.csv`: recommended calorie and macro targets
- `water_plan.csv`: hydration targets by time of day

## Recommended use

1. Use the exercise list to structure a 4-6 day training week.
2. Aim for 1.6-2.2 g of protein per kg of body weight.
3. Eat a small calorie surplus of about 250-400 kcal above maintenance.
4. Sleep 7-9 hours and keep rest days in place.
5. Drink 2.5-3.5 liters of water daily, more if training hard in heat.

## Best-practice summary

For the most muscle possible, focus on progressive overload, adequate protein, consistent training, and enough sleep. A lean bulk is usually better than a large bulk because it helps you gain more muscle with less fat gain.
